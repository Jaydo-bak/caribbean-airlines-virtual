About the Boeing 737-8 MAX

For Caribbean Airlines Virtual, the Boeing 737-8 MAX is the primary jet aircraft for routes linking Trinidad and Tobago with destinations across the Caribbean, North America and South America. It offers a modern glass cockpit, efficient engines and the performance needed for both regional and international sectors.
